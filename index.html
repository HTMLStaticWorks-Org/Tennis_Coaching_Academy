High Performance Methodology

The Vantage Coaching Method

We combine European clay court endurance, modern kinetic biomechanics, and collegiate match psychology to develop complete, adaptable tennis players capable of competing on any surface.

Kinetic Biomechanics

240fps slow-motion analysis optimizing kinetic chain & racket head speed

Footwork & Agility

Split-step timing, lateral slide recovery, and micro-adjustment steps

Tactical Geometry

Serve-plus-one patterns, crosscourt depth, and angle exploitation

Mental Toughness

Pressure simulation, break point composure, and between-point rituals

Conditioning & Core

Rotational power, rotator cuff durability, and injury prevention

Verified Match Play

Weekly UTR verified events & NCAA collegiate scout showcase circuits
