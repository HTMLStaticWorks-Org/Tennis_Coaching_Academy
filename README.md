World-Class Tennis Training Sanctuary & Athletic Academy.

## Overview
- **Brand:** Vantage Tennis Academy
- **Theme Palette:** Championship Wimbledon Green (`#0B3323`), Optic Tennis Lime (`#B8EB18`), Roland Garros Terracotta (`#B8532F`), Trophy Gold (`#CCA73A`), Crisp Court Off-White (`#F7FAF7`), Night Tournament Dark (`#0A1410`).

  - `training.html` - Training: Interactive photo gallery with 15 tennis photos, filter categories, telemetry labs, match play.

  - `login.html` & `signup.html` - Student portal authentication.
